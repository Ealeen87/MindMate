The Challenge

Alzheimer's Patients Face Daily Risks

Millions of families deal with life-threatening challenges every day. Current solutions are either too expensive, too complex, or simply not enough.

🚶
Wandering & Getting Lost

Alzheimer's patients frequently wander away from home, putting themselves in immediate danger and causing family panic.

💊
Missed Medications

Forgetting critical medications is common and can quickly lead to serious health complications or emergencies.

🩺
Fall Risk

Falls are a leading cause of injury in elderly patients. Without detection, help may arrive far too late.

😰
Caregiver Stress

Constant supervision is emotionally and physically exhausting. Caregivers can't watch their loved one every single minute.

💸
Expensive Solutions

Most existing monitoring solutions require Wi-Fi, are costly, or are too technically complex for elderly patients to use.
